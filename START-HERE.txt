DOMINO NIGHT - EVENT TRACKER

OPEN THE APP
On this Windows laptop, double-click "Open Tracker.cmd".
It starts a local-only helper and opens the tracker in your default browser.
If Windows blocks the launcher, open "Domino-Night.html" directly in Chrome or Edge.
The HTML file is self-contained and works offline; no account or subscription is needed.
Always use the same opening method, browser, and file location during the event.
The localhost address and a directly opened file have separate saved browser data.
Use Export backup / Restore backup to move between them.

REGISTRATION
1. Set the event name and available table count (default: six).
2. Adjust the number of players (8-48), then click Apply count.
3. Replace Player 1, Player 2, etc. with names, one per line. Use unique names.
4. Click Save names. When registration closes, click Generate qualifying rounds.
5. Print all rounds and the updated rules from inside the app.
After generation you may rename players on their original lines, but you cannot
add/remove/reorder participants without starting a new event. Do not shuffle the
name list after play begins: results belong to the corresponding roster slots.

PLAY AND SCORE
Every match is a race to 100 or more. Enter the final score for both teams.
Overshoots count in full; both teammates receive the entire team score.
Each round has floor(players / 4) matches. Any remaining players receive byes.
Byes rotate evenly and do not count toward the four-match minimum. Extra rounds ensure
every player plays at least four qualifying matches. Standings use win percentage,
then average points per played match.
If more matches are needed than tables available, play the numbered waves in order.
Finish every match in one round before starting the next.

TV DISPLAY
Use a second display connected to this laptop by HDMI or screen casting.
Click Open TV display, move that browser window to the TV, and select Full screen.
The display updates from the organizer tab and cycles through standings pages every 12 seconds.
Keep both tabs in the same browser/profile on this computer.
This is not a networked phone-scoring app. A separate device does not receive live data.
If your browser blocks a popup, allow this local app's TV window.
If direct-file tabs do not sync, export a backup, use Open Tracker.cmd, restore the
backup there, and open the TV view from that version.

PLAYOFFS
Finish all scheduled qualifying rounds. Every player must have played at least four matches. Review all results in Standings.
Publicly click Draw ties & create playoffs. Exact ties are randomly resolved once;
the saved draw record appears in Standings. Refreshing never repeats the draw.
Top eight: semifinal A is (1+8) vs (4+5); semifinal B is (2+7) vs (3+6).
The two winners play the final, with the same partners and scores starting at zero.
Correcting a qualifying result after the cut clears playoffs after a confirmation.
Correcting a semifinal rebuilds the final and may clear its result after confirmation.

BACKUPS
Keep only one organizer tab open. TV tabs are read-only.
Export a backup after each round and after the final. Keep it outside the browser.
The app automatically downloads a backup before the playoff cut or resetting results.
Use Restore backup to recover or move the event to another computer/browser.
CSV exports are for viewing standings; they cannot restore an event.
Do not use private/incognito browsing for the event or clear browser site data.

RULES UPDATE
The Rules & help screen is the current version: at least four played matches per
player, rotating byes, and percentage/average ranking. Older three-round events can
use Add remaining qualifying rounds to append matches while preserving earlier
qualifying pairings and results. Extending clears any old playoffs after confirmation.
The host must still announce the specific domino variant and house gameplay rules.
